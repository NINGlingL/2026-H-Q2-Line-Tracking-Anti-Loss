# Ball-on-Beam/Ball-and-Plate PID Control with STM32 and Camera-Based Position Detection

## Overview

Multiple research projects and implementations demonstrate successful ball-balancing systems using **STM32 microcontrollers**, **camera-based position detection**, and **PID control**. These systems generally involve a ball rolling on a beam or plate, where a camera detects the ball's position in real time and a PID controller adjusts servo motors to tilt the surface and keep the ball balanced.

---

## Common System Architecture

A typical setup splits processing across components:

- **Vision/Detection**: A camera captures the ball's position. Common approaches include a USB camera with OpenCV-Python on a PC, a Raspberry Pi camera module with Hough transform detection, or a camera board directly attached to the STM32.
- **Control Processing**: The **STM32** microcontroller (commonly STM32F4 series — e.g., STM32F407, STM32F401, STM32F103) runs the **PID control algorithm** and generates PWM signals for servos.
- **Actuation**: Two RC servo motors tilt the plate/beam in pitch and roll axes (for 2-DOF plate systems) or a single servo controls beam angle (for 1-DOF beam systems).

---

## Camera-Based Position Detection Approaches

1. **Color Thresholding + Center of Mass** (STM32F4 with onboard camera): The most hardware-efficient method. A dark ball on a white surface is detected by thresholding RGB values (e.g., R,G,B all below calibrated thresholds). The ball position is computed as the center of mass of all "ball" pixels. Full-frame scanning on an STM32F4 takes ~2.4 seconds, so **adaptive region-of-interest (ROI) scanning** is used — scanning only a small window around the last known ball position reduces processing time to ~0.15 seconds, enabling a **5 Hz sampling rate (0.2 s period)**.

2. **OpenCV + USB Camera + PC**: A USB camera feeds video to a PC running OpenCV-Python for ball detection. The coordinate is then transmitted to the STM32 via serial. This offloads heavy image processing from the microcontroller.

3. **Hough Transform on Raspberry Pi**: A Raspberry Pi camera and the Hough transform algorithm detect the ball at **15 FPS**, with position data sent to an STM32F407 for PID computation and motor control.

# 电赛中基于OpenMV的板球/滚球控制系统技术总结

## 系统概述

您搜索的关键词指向的是**全国大学生电子设计竞赛（电赛）中经典的"滚球控制系统"（板球系统/Ball-on-Plate System）**。这是一个典型的二维非线性、欠驱动闭环控制系统，核心任务是利用视觉反馈实时控制钢球在平板（或管道）上的位置。该系统融合了**机器视觉感知、舵机控制、PID算法**三大核心技术。

---

## 1. 系统架构

系统采用**"感知—决策—执行—反馈"**四层闭环结构：

| 层级 | 功能 | 核心器件 |
|------|------|----------|
| **感知层** | 图像采集与钢球位置检测 | OpenMV摄像头模块 |
| **决策层** | 坐标接收与PID控制运算 | STM32F103/F4系列主控 |
| **执行层** | 倾斜平台角度调节 | 两自由度舵机（X/Y轴） |
| **反馈层** | 实时坐标回传 | UART/蓝牙串口通信 |

---

## 2. OpenMV视觉位置检测

### 2.1 识别策略选择
- **推荐方案：黑板+白球**。经实测，白板黑球因灯光反射和阴影会产生噪点，而**黑色底板配合白色小球**识别效果最佳（使用黑色自动喷漆处理亚克力板）。
- 设定LAB颜色阈值，通过`img.find_blobs()`寻找最大色块，用`blob.cx()`和`blob.cy()`获取钢球的像素坐标中心。

### 2.2 图像处理流程
```
图像采集 → 灰度化/二值化（Otsu自适应阈值法）→ 连通域分析 →
轮廓提取 → 几何中心拟合 → 亚像素级坐标解算
```

### 2.3 坐标映射
需进行**像素坐标到物理坐标的标定**（使用棋盘格或已知尺寸参照物进行单目相机标定），建立毫米级空间定位模型，补偿镜头畸变，确保位置反馈误差控制在±0.5mm以内。
